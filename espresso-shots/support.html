Espresso Shots: Rude Awakening

Get in touch

Found a bug, lost progress or have a question? Email melgameproductions@gmail.com.

It helps if you include your iPhone model, your iOS version and the game’s version (shown at the bottom of the game’s Settings), plus what happened just before the problem.

Common questions

My purchases are missing

Open the Shop and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account you bought them with.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I turn off play data?

In the game: Settings → Share play data. See the privacy policy for what it sends.

How do I change my ad choices?

Personalised ads: your iPhone’s Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EU, UK and Switzerland, also the game’s Settings → Ad privacy choices.

How do I start over?

In the game: Settings → Reset progress. It erases your progress but keeps your purchases.
